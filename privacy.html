Momo Reef — Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Momo Reef keeps what you write on your phone. Your habits, moods, journal entries and progress are stored on the device and are never uploaded to us. You do not need an account. Subscription services process the limited data described below.

What stays on your device

The following information stays in the app’s local storage:

  • Your habits and whether you completed them
  • Your turtle's name and how it looks
  • Mood check-ins and journal reflections
  • Energy, pearls, trips and the things your turtle found
  • Your settings, including which reminders you turned on

This is held in the app's own storage. It is included in an encrypted device backup if you use one, and it is deleted when you delete the app. Because there is no account, this also means the data cannot be recovered by us if the app or the device is lost.

What leaves your device

Two things, and only these.

Subscriptions

We use RevenueCat to load subscription offers and check purchase status. It connects when the app opens, including for free users, using a randomly generated app user identifier and technical information needed to provide the service. When you purchase or restore a subscription, Apple and RevenueCat process purchase records and subscription status. RevenueCat also provides subscription analytics. We do not send RevenueCat your habit text, journal entries, moods, turtle name, or email address, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

Problem reports

If you tap “Report a problem”, the app opens your email app with a message already written. It contains the app version, your iOS version, your chosen theme, and whether you are a subscriber. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and you can edit or delete any of it first. If sent, we receive your email address and the contents of your message to respond to your request.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled by your phone, on your phone. There is no push server, and no notification token is created or transmitted. If you decline notification permission the app carries on working.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync
  • No advertising, advertising identifiers, or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites
  • No separate behavioural analytics service
  • We do not sell your personal information. Subscription processing uses the services described above.

Children

Momo Reef is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them beyond the service processing described above. Please contact us with concerns about a child’s information.

Your control

Deleting the app removes its local progress, but does not cancel a subscription or delete records held by Apple or RevenueCat. Contact us about privacy requests relating to subscription or support records. You can turn the evening reminder off in the app, or disable all notifications in iOS Settings. For anything relating to a subscription, Apple holds the purchase record and you can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
