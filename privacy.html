Momo Reef — Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

Momo Reef keeps what you write on your phone. Your habits, moods, journal entries and progress are stored on the device and are never uploaded to us. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

What stays on your device

All of it. Specifically:

  • Your habits and whether you completed them
  • Your turtle's name and how it looks
  • Mood check-ins and journal reflections
  • Energy, pearls, trips and the things your turtle found
  • Your settings, including which reminders you turned on

This is held in the app's own storage. It is included in an encrypted device backup if you use one, and it is deleted when you delete the app. Because there is no account, this also means the data cannot be recovered by us if the app or the device is lost.

What leaves your device

Two things, and only these.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, the purchase is handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, which we use to check whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated identifier and the receipt Apple issues for the purchase. We do not send it your name, your email, or anything you have written in the app, and the app does not ask RevenueCat to collect device identifiers. If you never subscribe, nothing is sent at all.

Problem reports

If you tap “Report a problem”, the app opens your email app with a message already written. It contains the app version, your iOS version, your chosen theme, and whether you are a subscriber. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and you can edit or delete any of it first.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled by your phone, on your phone. There is no push server, and no notification token is created or transmitted. If you decline notification permission the app carries on working.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs beyond the subscription handling described above
  • No selling or sharing of personal information — there is none to sell

Children

Momo Reef is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. As described above, it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Your control

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings, or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings. For anything relating to a subscription, Apple holds the purchase record and you can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
