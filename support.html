Momo Reef — Support

A self-care habit tracker for iPhone

Something broken, or a question? Email fenua.ora.contact@gmail.com and you will get a reply from a person.

The fastest way to report a problem

In the app, open Settings (the gear, top right) and tap “Something broken? Report a problem” at the bottom. That writes the email for you with the app version and your iOS version already filled in, which usually saves a round trip. Nothing is sent until you press send, and you can edit any of it first.

Common questions

Where is my data?

On your phone, and only there. There is no account and no cloud, which also means it cannot be recovered if the app is deleted or the phone is lost. See the privacy policy.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Not yet. Each device keeps its own reef. A subscription follows your Apple ID, so “Restore purchases” will unlock a second device even though the progress does not carry across.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In the iOS Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple handles billing, so cancelling is done there rather than in Momo Reef.

My turtle looks unwell and I have been keeping up

Health follows the last seven days, so it takes a few days of showing up to climb back. If it looks wrong rather than merely low, please report it — with a screenshot if you can.

I did not get my reminders

Check notifications are allowed for Momo Reef in iOS Settings. The evening nudge is deliberately conditional: it is only sent if the day's one thing is still undone.
